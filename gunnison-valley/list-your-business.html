List your business, or update your listing

Farms, ranches, markets and food makers in the Gunnison Valley can be listed at no cost. Already on the map? Use this same form to correct your hours, season, contact details or products.

How it works

  1. You fill in this form. Hours and season are included, so customers know when to come.
  2. It goes into the producers data set. The form feeds the same Colorado producers data set that this map is built from, so one submission is the single source for the map and anything else built on that data.
  3. A person reviews it. We check it against your own website or social page, and merge it with any existing listing so there are no duplicates.
  4. It goes live fast. Typically within a few days, and within a month at most.
  5. Your fix sticks. A correction you send is saved, so a later data refresh never overwrites it.

Separately, the whole data set is re-checked and refreshed at least monthly. See how monthly verification works.
